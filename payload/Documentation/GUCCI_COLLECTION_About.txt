GUCCI COLLECTION
TWO INSTRUMENTS. ONE SIGNATURE SOUND.

ZAY KEYS — Vintage Keyboard Instrument
Bring classic keyboard-inspired sounds into your productions.

ZAY KEYS is a virtual instrument built around a keyboard-focused experience, inspired by the melodic identity of classic Trap and Hip-Hop production. Its purpose is to give producers a dedicated space for creating melodies, chords, progressions, and musical ideas with a distinctive vintage-inspired character.

Designed for:
- Melodies and chord progressions
- Trap and Hip-Hop production
- Vintage-inspired keyboard sounds
- Building musical ideas and arrangements

Classic keys. Southern vibes. Timeless melodies.

MPC3000 SIMULATOR — Vintage Sampling Instrument
Bring a classic sampling-inspired workflow into your studio.

MPC3000 SIMULATOR is a virtual instrument inspired by the legendary MPC3000 and its place in Hip-Hop production history. It is designed around a sampling-oriented experience, giving producers a dedicated environment for working with samples, creating drum patterns, and exploring classic beat-making approaches.

Designed for:
- Sample-based production
- Drum patterns and beat construction
- Classic Hip-Hop-inspired workflows
- Vintage-inspired production aesthetics

Classic sampling. Raw drums. Legendary groove.

INSTALLATION
Select one or both instruments. The default VST3 destination is:
C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3

After installation, open your DAW and scan for new plugins if they do not appear automatically.
